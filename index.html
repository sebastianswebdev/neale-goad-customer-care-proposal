Workshop attachment

Try the whole conversation.

  1. Customer: open a service record, view its photos, and add a question.
  2. Workshop: switch the demo view above, reply to the question, or open Post vehicle update.
  3. Post an update: record a service date, work carried out, a sample photo and an optional next recommendation.
  4. Customer: switch back. The update and reply are in the same vehicle’s history.
  5. Request work: ask for a service or repair. Review and reply to it in the workshop view.

Everything is fictional and stored on this browser only. No real customer information should be entered. Reset demo clears this prototype’s records, comments and uploaded photos on this device.
